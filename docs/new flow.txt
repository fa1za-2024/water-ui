Step 01: (A) Wake up from Deep Sleep (Timer set to 30 seconds).
Step 02: Measure Battery Voltage.
Step 03: Decision: Is battery less than 15%?
	YES: Proceed to Step 04.
	NO: Proceed to Step 05.
Step 04: (B) Go to Deep Sleep (End of current cycle, preserve battery).
Step 05: Turn on the sensors (via GPIO 7. Ensure analog sensors use ADC1 pins).
Step 06: Wait 5 seconds for sensors stabilization.
Step 07: Set Sample Counter to 0.
Step 08: Decision: Is sample counter less than 50?
	YES: Proceed to Step 09.
	NO: Proceed to Step 12.
Step 09: Read Analog Sensor (ADC1: GPIO 1 & 2 for pH/Turbidity).
Step 10: Wait for 20 ms.
Step 11: Add 1 to Sample Counter. Return to Step 08.
Step 12: Sort the array from lowest to highest. Filter out NaN or disconnected-sensor values. Discard the top 10 and bottom 10 readings.
Step 13: Calculate average value for pH and Turbidity (Sum of remaining 30 middle samples / 30).
Step 13.5: Decision: Are calculated values within physical limits? (e.g., pH 0-14).
	YES: Proceed to Step 14.
	NO: Proceed to Step 04 (B) (Discard invalid reading and go to sleep).
Step 14: Turn off sensors.
Step 15: (C) Set wifi_try Counter to 0.
Step 16: Initialize Wi-Fi Connection with 10-second timeout.
Step 17: Decision: Is Wi-Fi connected?
	YES: Proceed to Step 20.
	NO: Proceed to Step 18.
Step 18: Decision: Is wifi_try Counter less than 3?
	YES: Proceed to Step 19.
	NO: Proceed to Step 19.5.
Step 19: Add 1 to wifi_try Counter. Return to Step 16.
Step 19.5: (NEW) Save current JSON payload to LittleFS (/backlog/<ts>.json). Apply circular buffer limit. Proceed to Step 04 (B).
Step 20: Synchronize NTP Time (Required to populate the ts epoch milliseconds field accurately).
Step 20.5: Retrieve RSSI via WiFi.RSSI().
Step 21: Set mqtt_try Counter to 0. Configure Last Will and Testament (LWT).
Step 22: Initialize MQTT Connection with 5-second timeout. Publish retained online status.
Step 23: Decision: Is MQTT connected?
	YES: Proceed to Step 26.
	NO: Proceed to Step 24.
Step 24: Decision: Is mqtt_try Counter less than 3?
	YES: Proceed to Step 25.
	NO: Proceed to Step 25.5.
Step 25: Add 1 to mqtt_try Counter. Return to Step 22.
Step 26: Save current JSON payload to LittleFS (/backlog/<ts>.json). 
		Apply circular buffer limit. Proceed to Step 04 (B).
Step 26: Scan LittleFS /backlog/ directory for any saved .json files. Sort them chronologically (oldest first).
Step 27: Build current JSON payload (using current readings and ts).
Step 28: Send current payload to MQTT Broker. Wait 500ms for network buffer to clear.
Step 29: Loop through the backlogged files from Step 26. Send each file's payload to MQTT Broker. Wait 500ms between each.
Step 30: (NEW) After all backlogged files are successfully transmitted, delete them from LittleFS (LittleFS.remove(filename)).
Step 31: (B) Go to Deep Sleep (Set timer for 30 seconds).
Step 32: (A) Wake up from Deep Sleep (Start of next cycle).
